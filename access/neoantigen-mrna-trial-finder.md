# Neoantigen And mRNA Trial Finder

Status: first-pass tool
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: trial landscape review, patient question preparation, clinician discussion support, not medical advice.

## Purpose

This tool helps find legitimate mRNA, neoantigen, cancer vaccine, TCR, and related immunotherapy trials without drifting into DIY vaccine design.

## Search Anchors

- NCI clinical trials search: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/
- Cancer center trial pages.
- Disease-specific foundation trial finders when reputable.
- Treating oncologist or trial office confirmation.

## Search Terms

Use combinations of:

- Cancer type and subtype.
- Stage or setting: metastatic, recurrent, refractory, adjuvant, neoadjuvant, minimal residual disease.
- Biomarker: MSI, TMB-high, PD-L1, EGFR, ALK, KRAS, BRAF, BRCA, HRD, HLA, specific mutation.
- Strategy: neoantigen, personalized vaccine, mRNA vaccine, RNA-lipoplex, dendritic cell vaccine, TCR, TIL, CAR-T, peptide vaccine, cancer vaccine.
- Product name if known: V940, intismeran autogene, autogene cevumeran, BNT111, BNT122, RO7198457, mRNA-4157.

## Questions Before Contacting A Trial

- Is the trial open at a site the patient could realistically reach?
- Is the patient's cancer type and stage included?
- Are prior treatments compatible?
- Are biomarker requirements known?
- Is measurable disease required?
- Is the trial for humans, not veterinary or preclinical work?
- Is this a real trial with an NCT number or institutional listing?
- What standard treatment would be delayed or replaced?

## Rosie-Specific Lesson

Rosie's case involved a bespoke veterinary experiment with researchers and oversight. For humans, the closest safe analogue is not making a vaccine. It is finding a legitimate trial or expert-led review pathway that already has clinical infrastructure.
