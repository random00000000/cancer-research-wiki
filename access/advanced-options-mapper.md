# Advanced Options Mapper

Status: first-pass tool
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: advanced-options navigation, clinician discussion support, research support, not medical advice.

## Purpose

This page maps what can be considered when standard options are failing or incomplete. It does not choose treatment. It gives a structured way to ask qualified teams what paths are legitimate.

## Option Categories

| Category | What to ask | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard care not yet used | Are there guideline-supported surgery, radiation, systemic therapy, biomarker, or supportive-care options still available? | Highest practical priority when applicable. |
| Molecularly matched therapy | Does the tumor have an actionable alteration linked to approved drugs or trials? | Depends on cancer type, alteration, evidence level, and access. |
| Molecular tumor board | Can a multidisciplinary team review genomic results and match options? | Recommendation is not automatic treatment. |
| Clinical trial | Is there an open trial matching cancer type, stage, biomarkers, prior therapy, location, and health status? | Research, not guaranteed benefit. |
| Expanded access / compassionate use | Can a treating physician request access to an investigational product when trial enrollment is not possible? | Requires physician, company agreement, FDA/IRB steps as applicable. |
| Off-label evidence review | Is there credible evidence for off-label use that the treating team would consider? | Must be clinician-led and payer/ethics-aware. |
| mRNA / neoantigen trial | Is there a legitimate trial requiring tumor sequencing, HLA, tissue, manufacturing, and monitoring? | Trial only unless approved; no DIY design. |
| Cell therapy / TIL / CAR-T / TCR | Does the cancer type and antigen/biomarker fit a real trial or approved indication? | Often intensive, eligibility-limited, and toxicity-monitored. |
| Repeat biopsy / resistance testing | Has the tumor changed in a way that creates new options? | Useful only when clinically safe and actionable. |

## Decision Questions

- Is the patient stable enough to wait for testing or trial screening?
- What standard options remain?
- What biomarker results already exist?
- What test could change the next decision?
- Is there enough tissue?
- Is there measurable disease?
- What prior treatments exclude or include the patient?
- Is the trial local, regional, national, or remote-screenable?
- What costs, travel, lodging, and monitoring are required?
- Who is the physician of record?

## Expanded Access Questions

- Is the product investigational and unavailable outside a trial?
- Why is trial enrollment not possible?
- Is there enough preliminary evidence to justify risk?
- Will the company provide the product?
- Will a licensed physician sponsor and monitor the request?
- What FDA form, IRB review, consent, and safety reporting are required?
- What costs are billed to the patient or insurer?

## Safety Boundary

This mapper is for expert discussions. It does not advise a patient to use any drug, vaccine, cell therapy, off-label treatment, or investigational product.
