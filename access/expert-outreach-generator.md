# Expert Outreach Generator

Status: first-pass tool
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: patient question preparation, advanced-options navigation, clinician discussion support, not medical advice.

## Purpose

The Rosie story worked because a person did not stop at internet research. He contacted people with real infrastructure. This page turns that into safe human outreach templates.

## Who To Contact

- Treating oncologist.
- Second-opinion cancer center.
- Molecular tumor board or precision oncology program.
- Trial office for a specific NCT record.
- Sequencing/genomics program attached to a cancer center.
- Disease-specific researcher, only after the clinical packet is clear.
- Expanded-access office or company medical information contact, with the treating physician involved.

## Email To A Cancer Center Or Molecular Tumor Board

```text
Subject: Request for advanced options review: [cancer type], [stage], [key biomarker if known]

Hello,

I am seeking a clinician-supervised advanced options review for a patient with [cancer type/subtype], [stage/current status], after [prior treatments]. We are not asking for remote treatment instructions. We are asking whether your team can review the case for standard remaining options, biomarker-driven therapy, molecular tumor board review, clinical trials, or expanded-access discussion if appropriate.

Available records:
- Pathology report: yes/no
- Recent imaging report and images: yes/no
- Biomarker/tumor sequencing report: yes/no
- Treatment timeline: yes/no
- Current oncology note: yes/no

Key question:
[One or two sentences: e.g., progression after X; actionable mutation Y; looking for trial or molecular tumor board review.]

Could you tell us:
1. Whether your team can review this case.
2. Which records are required.
3. Whether remote record review is possible.
4. Whether a molecular tumor board or trial office should receive the packet.
5. Whether urgent standard care should proceed while review is pending.

Thank you.
```

## Email To A Trial Team

```text
Subject: Eligibility question for [NCT number] - [cancer type], [biomarker], [prior therapy]

Hello,

I am asking whether this patient may be worth formal screening for [trial title / NCT number]. The treating oncology team can provide records if there is a plausible match.

Cancer type/subtype:
Stage/current status:
Key biomarkers:
Prior treatments:
Performance status if known:
Location/travel limits:
Recent scan date:

Questions:
1. Is the trial currently recruiting at your site?
2. Are the listed biomarkers/prior treatments potentially compatible?
3. Is fresh biopsy, measurable disease, HLA typing, leukapheresis, or sequencing required?
4. What records should the treating oncologist send?
5. Are routine-care costs, travel, lodging, or screening costs addressed by the protocol or site?

We understand trial participation is research and not guaranteed treatment benefit.
```

## Email To A Researcher

```text
Subject: Clinician-supervised question about [cancer type / biomarker / trial area]

Hello,

I read your work on [paper/topic]. I am helping organize questions for a clinician-supervised review of a patient with [cancer type] and [biomarker/context]. We are not seeking treatment instructions, dosing, manufacturing, or self-administration guidance.

Could you point us toward:
1. Relevant clinical trials or trial groups.
2. Molecular tumor board programs familiar with this mechanism.
3. Key papers that define whether this biomarker is actionable.
4. Known reasons this approach fails or is unsafe.

The treating oncologist will make clinical decisions.
```

## Outreach Rules

- Attach a concise case summary, not a data dump.
- Never ask researchers to prescribe outside a clinical relationship.
- Ask for routing, evidence, trials, and expert review.
- Keep the treating oncologist in the loop.
- Track who was contacted, date, response, next step, and records requested.
