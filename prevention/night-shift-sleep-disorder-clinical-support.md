## Plain-Language Summary

Night work can make almost anyone tired. But severe insomnia, repeated dangerous sleepiness, near-misses while driving, falling asleep unintentionally, loud snoring or breathing pauses, mood collapse, medication-related sedation, or pregnancy-related fatigue should not be dismissed as "just shift work."

Shift Work Disorder is a circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorder where insomnia and/or excessive sleepiness are tied to a work schedule that overlaps the usual sleep period. It is not diagnosed just because a person dislikes nights. The practical public message is: protect sleep and schedules where possible, but get clinical or occupational support when fatigue becomes dangerous, persistent, or medically complicated.

## What Is Strong Enough For Public Education

- Shift Work Disorder is a recognized circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorder involving insomnia and/or excessive sleepiness linked to work hours.
- It affects a minority of shift workers, not every night worker.
- Night workers can also have other sleep problems, including sleep apnea or chronic insomnia, that need different evaluation.
- Medication, melatonin, light therapy, caffeine timing, and wake-promoting drugs are not one-size-fits-all public advice; they require careful timing, risk review, and clinician involvement when symptoms are serious.
- Employers should make support usable: predictable scheduling, fatigue reporting, rest spaces, occupational-health referral, and accommodations without punishment.

## Public Wording

Use:

- "Dangerous sleepiness is a safety signal, not a weakness."
- "Shift Work Disorder is a medical sleep-wake disorder, not a character flaw."
- "Sleep apnea symptoms, severe insomnia, sedating medicines, pregnancy, or near-misses need support."
- "Employers should provide fatigue reporting, rest options, schedule review, and referral paths."

Avoid:

- "Everyone on nights has Shift Work Disorder."
- "Just take melatonin."
- "Wake-promoting drugs are a cancer-prevention tool."
- "If you cannot handle nights, it is your fault."

## Source Cards

### Frontiers 2021: Prevalence Of Shift Work Disorder

- URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2021.638252/full
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2021.638252
- Source type: systematic review / meta-analysis
- Reusable claims:
  - Shift Work Disorder prevalence estimates vary widely by definition and population.
  - The review supports describing Shift Work Disorder as affecting a meaningful minority of shift workers rather than all shift workers.
- Caution:
  - Different diagnostic tools and occupational groups produce different estimates.

### Scientific Reports 2023: Undiagnosed Sleep Disorders And Mental Health In Shift Workers

- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-41129-9
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41129-9
- Source type: observational sleep and mental-health study
- Reusable claims:
  - Shift workers can have undiagnosed sleep disorders and mental-health burden.
  - Screening and referral matter when symptoms are persistent or severe.
- Caution:
  - Observational evidence cannot prove every symptom is caused by the work schedule.

### Frontiers 2021: Sleep Disorders In Night Shift And Rotating Shift Workers

- URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fneur.2021.638252/full
- Source type: review
- Reusable claims:
  - Night and rotating shift workers can experience insomnia, excessive sleepiness, sleep debt, and comorbid sleep disorders.
  - Clinical assessment should separate schedule-related circadian misalignment from other sleep conditions.
- Caution:
  - Public guidance should not collapse all sleep disorders into a single shift-work label.

### StatPearls: Shift Work Sleep Disorder

- URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK589670/
- Source type: clinical overview
- Reusable claims:
  - Shift Work Sleep Disorder is described within circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders.
  - Assessment includes work schedule, sleep symptoms, safety risks, and differential diagnosis.
- Caution:
  - This is a broad clinical overview and should be paired with primary reviews and sleep-medicine sources.

### Cleveland Clinic: Shift Work Sleep Disorder

- URL: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/12146-shift-work-sleep-disorder
- Source type: clinical public education
- Reusable claims:
  - Symptoms can include insomnia and excessive sleepiness related to shift schedules.
  - Professional evaluation is appropriate when symptoms are persistent or interfere with safety and functioning.
- Caution:
  - Use as a public-facing explanation, not as the evidence base for prevalence or treatment effect sizes.
