# Source Card: Autogene Cevumeran Personalized mRNA-Lipoplex Trials

Status: source card
Last updated: 2026-06-14

## Metadata

- Product names: autogene cevumeran, BNT122, RO7198457
- Source type: phase 1 clinical trial reports and follow-up reports
- Advanced solid tumor report: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03334-7
- Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma follow-up: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08508-4
- Advanced solid tumor trial identifier: NCT03289962
- Pancreatic phase 2 trial identifier: NCT05968326
- Colorectal phase 2 trial identifier: NCT04486378
- Platform: individualized uridine mRNA lipoplex encoding patient-specific neoantigens

## Main Findings

- In the advanced solid tumor phase 1 report, autogene cevumeran was designed from each patient's tumor-specific somatic mutation data to stimulate T-cell responses against up to 20 neoantigens.
- The report describes treatment as monotherapy and with atezolizumab, with safety/tolerability as the primary objective and immunogenicity/preliminary activity as exploratory objectives.
- The abstract reports neoantigen-specific CD4+ and/or CD8+ T-cell responses in 71% of patients in a non-prespecified interim analysis.
- In the pancreatic cancer follow-up, durable CD8+ T-cell clones and longer recurrence-free survival were reported in immune responders, but the dataset was very small and needs randomized confirmation.

## Claims Safe To Reuse

- Autogene cevumeran is important because it shows that individualized mRNA-lipoplex vaccines can generate measurable neoantigen-specific T-cell responses in humans.
- Pancreatic cancer results are scientifically important because PDAC is usually hard to immunize against, but the clinical evidence remains early.
- Manufacturing timing, tissue quality, immune fitness, tumor burden, and combination therapy are central to whether this approach can work.

## Claims Requiring Caution

- The advanced solid tumor report was not primarily designed to prove clinical efficacy.
- Small responder/non-responder analyses can generate hypotheses but should not be framed as definitive proof of survival benefit.
- Do not present autogene cevumeran as an approved or broadly available pancreatic cancer cure.
