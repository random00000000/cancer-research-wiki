## Main Findings

- mRNA cancer vaccine trials can measure biological immune engagement through neoantigen-specific CD4+ and CD8+ T-cell responses.
- Autogene cevumeran PDAC reports are important because they describe durable neoantigen-specific CD8+ T-cell clones and an association between response and delayed recurrence in a small early dataset.
- A 2025 individualized mRNA vaccine report in triple-negative breast cancer describes durable, functional T-cell responses to multiple neoantigens in a small post-surgical cohort.
- Reviews on cancer vaccine trial evaluation emphasize that immune-response measures are scientifically useful, but validated immunological surrogates for clinical benefit remain challenging.

## Claims Safe To Reuse

- A measured T-cell response means the vaccine engaged immune biology.
- Durable, functional T-cell responses are more meaningful than a transient or poorly characterized response.
- Clinical endpoints such as recurrence-free survival, disease-free survival, progression-free survival, overall survival, response durability, and safety still decide whether a therapy helps patients.

## Claims Requiring Caution

- Do not say a T-cell response equals cure.
- Do not treat a small responder/non-responder analysis as proof of broad clinical efficacy.
- Do not compare immune assays across trials as if they were standardized unless the methods are comparable.
- Do not ignore tumor access, T-cell exhaustion, antigen loss, HLA loss, and microenvironment suppression.
