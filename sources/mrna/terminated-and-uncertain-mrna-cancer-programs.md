## Why This Card Exists

The mRNA frontier should track what fails or remains uncertain, not only what looks promising. A terminated, inconclusive, underpowered, unpublished, or sponsor-reported program can teach future agents where the biology, setting, trial design, or business case may be fragile.

## Main Findings

- NCT06295809 tested neoadjuvant and adjuvant intismeran autogene/V940 plus pembrolizumab in locally resectable advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma. The local tracker records it as terminated and flags it as a negative/uncertain signal requiring follow-up.
- BNT111 in advanced melanoma has a company-reported positive phase 2 topline signal with cemiplimab, but full peer-reviewed details, response durability, subgroup interpretation, and safety context should be crawled before strong claims are reused.
- Registry status is not the same as efficacy evidence. A trial can recruit without proving benefit, complete without published results, or terminate for reasons that require careful interpretation.

## Claims Safe To Reuse

- Trial failure, termination, or missing publication is part of the evidence landscape.
- mRNA cancer vaccine performance may be disease-setting specific; a melanoma adjuvant signal should not be copy-pasted onto every cancer.
- Sponsor-reported topline claims can guide crawl priority but should be labeled preliminary until full data are peer-reviewed or otherwise independently assessable.

## Claims Requiring Caution

- Do not infer from a terminated program alone that all personalized mRNA vaccines fail.
- Do not infer from a positive topline release alone that a product is clinically proven.
- Do not merge different products, delivery platforms, antigens, settings, endpoints, and combinations as if they were one therapy class with one effect size.
