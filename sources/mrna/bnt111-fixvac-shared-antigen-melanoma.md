# Source Card: BNT111 FixVac Shared-Antigen mRNA Vaccine In Melanoma

Status: source card
Last updated: 2026-06-14

## Metadata

- Product: BNT111
- Platform: FixVac, uridine mRNA-lipoplex
- Strategy: off-the-shelf/shared tumor-associated antigen vaccine
- Foundational peer-reviewed report: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32728218/
- 2024 company-reported phase 2 update: https://investors.biontech.de/news-releases/news-release-details/biontech-announces-positive-topline-phase-2-results-mrna/
- Trial identifier from company release: NCT04526899
- Cancer type: unresectable stage III or IV melanoma, including anti-PD-(L)1 relapsed/refractory setting

## Main Findings

- BNT111 targets a fixed set of non-mutated melanoma-associated antigens rather than sequencing a unique vaccine for every patient.
- The 2020 Nature report described melanoma FixVac as an intravenously administered liposomal RNA vaccine targeting four non-mutated tumor-associated antigens.
- BioNTech's 2024 company update reported that a phase 2 trial met its primary endpoint for overall response rate when BNT111 was combined with cemiplimab in anti-PD-(L)1 relapsed/refractory advanced melanoma.

## Claims Safe To Reuse

- Shared-antigen mRNA vaccines trade personalization for speed and off-the-shelf scalability.
- Shared antigens can be useful when many tumors express the same target, but they may be less tumor-specific than private neoantigens.
- Company-reported positive topline data should be tracked, but peer-reviewed results and full adverse-event tables are needed.

## Claims Requiring Caution

- A historical-control comparison is weaker than a randomized comparison against a concurrent control arm.
- Shared tumor-associated antigens can also appear in some normal or developmentally related tissues, so target biology and safety matter.
- Response rate in advanced disease is not the same endpoint as recurrence-free survival after surgery.

## Follow-Up Crawl Targets

- Full peer-reviewed NCT04526899 phase 2 publication.
- Antigen expression and HLA-presentation data for NY-ESO-1, MAGE-A3, tyrosinase, and TPTE.
- BNT111 combination and monotherapy arms, duration of response, progression-free survival, overall survival, and immune-related adverse events.
