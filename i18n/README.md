## Translation Rule

Translations should preserve medical safety boundaries. Do not make claims stronger than the English source, and do not turn research content into personal medical advice.

See [TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md](TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md) before adding or expanding translated pages.
