# Translation Guide

Status: first-pass multilingual safety guide
Last updated: 2026-06-12

This guide keeps translated Cancer Research Wiki pages useful, safe, and aligned with the evidence base.

## Canonical Source

The English files are the canonical research record unless a translated page explicitly states otherwise. When updating a translation, compare against the current English source rather than an older translation.

## Safety Boundaries To Preserve

Every translated entry page should preserve these boundaries:

- The wiki is for research, education, prevention literacy, and scientific reasoning.
- The wiki is not a doctor, diagnostic device, or treatment prescriber.
- Agents should not diagnose people or modify treatment plans.
- Experimental therapies must not be presented as proven.
- Personal questions about symptoms, diagnosis, staging, prognosis, treatment, supplements, clinical trials, or personal risk should direct people to qualified clinicians.

## Medical Translation Rules

Use conservative wording:

- Translate "lower risk" as risk reduction, not guaranteed prevention.
- Translate "screening" as checking before symptoms, not diagnosis.
- Translate "evidence suggests" without making it sound certain.
- Preserve "population-level" when a claim is about groups, not individuals.
- Preserve uncertainty, caveats, and source limitations.

Avoid:

- Miracle-cure language.
- Direct treatment instructions.
- Claims that cancer is fully controllable by lifestyle.
- Claims that all official sources are wrong or useless.
- Removing cautions because they make the text less smooth.

## Link Pattern

Translated entry pages should include:

- A language switcher at the top.
- The shared infographic image using `../visuals/cancer-literacy-infographic.svg`.
- Links to core evidence pages using `../`.
- A clear note that deeper source pages may still be in English.

## Review Checklist

Before committing a translated page:

- Links resolve from the `i18n/` directory.
- The infographic renders through the relative path.
- Medical safety wording is present.
- The three project goals are represented.
- mRNA content is labeled as research intelligence, not treatment advice.
- No translated sentence overstates the English evidence.

## Future Work

- Track translation review status by language and page.
