# Rosie Playbook Next Crawl

Status: active crawl queue
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: agent research planning, advanced cancer access, clinician discussion support, not medical advice.

## Purpose

Deepen the Rosie Playbook into a current, expert-led advanced-options toolkit for human cancer cases where standard options are failing or incomplete.

## High-Priority Crawl Targets

- Primary documentation or formal case write-up for Rosie if published.
- Interviews or institutional pages from UNSW, Ramaciotti Centre for Genomics, UNSW RNA Institute, and veterinary oncology collaborators that clarify what was actually done.
- Human molecular tumor board workflow papers, including intake requirements, variant interpretation, trial matching, and outcomes.
- FDA expanded access, right-to-try, emergency IND, IRB, informed consent, sponsor agreement, and physician responsibilities.
- NCI and cancer center precision oncology pages for genomic testing, molecular tumor boards, rare cancer clinics, and trial matching.
- ClinicalTrials.gov search strategies for neoantigen, mRNA, personalized vaccine, TCR, TIL, CAR-T, cell therapy, molecularly matched, basket, umbrella, and expanded-access studies.
- Evidence on off-label targeted therapy after genomic testing, including response rates, failure modes, and ethical cautions.
- Tumor-normal sequencing, RNA sequencing, HLA typing, ctDNA, repeat biopsy, and resistance-testing literature.
- Fraud and pay-to-play clinic warnings from FDA, FTC, NCI, state medical boards, and cancer centers.

## Data Fields To Capture

- Resource or paper title.
- URL / DOI / PMID / NCT / official identifier.
- Source type.
- What decision it helps with.
- Required patient data.
- Required clinician or institution.
- Costs or access barriers if described.
- Safety/ethics constraints.
- Claims safe to reuse.
- Claims requiring caution.
- Related playbook page.

## Tool Targets

- A machine-readable trial-search query builder.
- A molecular tumor board intake checklist.
- Expanded-access request readiness checklist for patient-facing preparation.
- Expert-outreach tracker table.
- Resistance-update packet template.
- Public "AI did not cure cancer; team infrastructure did" explainer.

## Safety Notes

- Do not provide DIY mRNA, vaccine, LNP, cell therapy, gene-editing, biologic, drug-compounding, dosing, procurement, or self-administration instructions.
- Do not imply expanded access is guaranteed.
- Do not imply a trial is a cure.
- Do not let the Rosie story become human efficacy evidence.
