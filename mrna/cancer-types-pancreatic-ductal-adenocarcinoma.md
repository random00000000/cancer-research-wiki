# mRNA Cancer Vaccines In Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

Status: disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, trial landscape review, patient question preparation, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma is one of the most important mRNA vaccine frontiers because it is usually difficult for the immune system to attack. Autogene cevumeran has produced early evidence of durable neoantigen-specific T-cell responses in some resected PDAC patients, but the evidence is still small and needs randomized phase 2 confirmation.

## Current Evidence

| Program | Setting | What it tests | Current reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Autogene cevumeran phase 1 follow-up | Resected PDAC | Individualized mRNA-lipoplex neoantigen vaccine with atezolizumab and mFOLFIRINOX context | Small but important T-cell durability signal; not definitive |
| NCT05968326 | Resected PDAC | Autogene cevumeran + atezolizumab + mFOLFIRINOX versus mFOLFIRINOX alone | Recruiting phase 2; primary endpoint disease-free survival |

## Why PDAC Is Hard

- PDAC often has dense stromal and immune-suppressive tumor microenvironment features.
- Disease can recur quickly, making manufacturing and clinical timing critical.
- Tumor samples, neoantigen quality, HLA presentation, and T-cell access may all limit benefit.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not call autogene cevumeran a pancreatic cancer cure.
- Do not infer responder analyses from a small early study prove survival benefit for all PDAC patients.
- Do not ignore the combination context: surgery, atezolizumab, chemotherapy, and vaccine biology are intertwined.
