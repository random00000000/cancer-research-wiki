# Antigen Presentation And Dendritic Cells In mRNA Cancer Vaccines

Status: mechanism page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, mechanism map, clinician discussion support, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

mRNA cancer vaccines do not directly "kill cancer." They try to teach immune cells what to look for. Dendritic cells and other antigen-presenting cells translate the mRNA instruction into protein fragments, display those fragments on MHC/HLA molecules, and help T cells decide whether to attack.

## The Teaching Chain

1. Delivery system brings mRNA into cells.
2. Cells translate mRNA into target protein fragments.
3. Antigen-processing machinery cuts proteins into peptides.
4. MHC class I presentation can activate CD8+ T cells that kill target-bearing cells.
5. MHC class II presentation can activate CD4+ helper T cells that support durable immunity.
6. Co-stimulation and inflammatory context decide whether T cells are activated, ignored, exhausted, or tolerized.

## Key Concepts

| Concept | Plain meaning | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dendritic cell | A professional immune teacher | Good T-cell priming often needs dendritic-cell help |
| MHC/HLA | The display platform for peptide fragments | A target matters only if it can be displayed |
| Cross-presentation | Presenting outside-derived antigens through MHC class I | Important for CD8+ T-cell priming |
| Co-stimulation | Second signal for T-cell activation | Without it, recognition may not become useful immunity |
| CD8+ T cell | Cancer-cell killing arm | Needs antigen display and tumor access |
| CD4+ T cell | Helper and organizer arm | Can support CD8+ function, memory, and immune architecture |

## Evidence Links

- Autogene cevumeran advanced solid tumor data reported poly-epitopic CD4+ and/or CD8+ neoantigen-specific responses in a phase 1 setting. See `sources/mrna/autogene-cevumeran-solid-tumors-and-pdac.md`.
- Delivery reviews emphasize that antigen-presenting cell access and innate immune context shape vaccine performance. See `sources/mrna/lnp-and-mrna-cancer-vaccine-delivery-reviews.md`.

## What Remains Uncertain

- Which dendritic-cell subsets matter most for each delivery route and cancer type.
- How much immune activation is helpful versus harmful.
- Which blood immune assays best reflect tumor-site T-cell function.
- Whether a measured T-cell response is sufficient, necessary, or merely correlated with benefit.

## Safe Interpretation

An immune response is evidence that the vaccine engaged biology. It is not automatically evidence of cure, survival benefit, or personal treatment success.
