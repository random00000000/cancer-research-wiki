# mRNA Track: Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

Status: first-pass disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: patient question preparation, trial landscape review, research hypothesis, not for clinical use.

## Summary

Triple-negative breast cancer is an early but important mRNA vaccine frontier because some tumors are genomically unstable and immunologically active enough to generate candidate neoantigens. The strongest local anchor is a small 2026 Nature report of individualized neoantigen mRNA vaccination after standard therapy, showing durable T-cell responses in 14 patients. This is promising biology, not standard treatment.

## Disease And Standard-Care Context

TNBC can relapse even after apparently successful local and systemic therapy. Modern care may include surgery, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, radiation, and germline/genomic testing depending on stage and biomarkers. mRNA vaccination, where studied, is an investigational layer added after or around standard care, not a replacement.

## mRNA Strategy Type

- Strategy: individualized neoantigen vaccine.
- Delivery: mRNA-lipoplex platform in the key source card.
- Intended setting: adjuvant/post-standard therapy immune surveillance.
- Intended immune response: durable CD4+ and CD8+ neoantigen-specific T-cell responses.

## What Is Known

- The 2026 TNBC report found vaccine-induced T-cell responses against multiple neoantigens in all 14 patients.
- Some responses persisted for years and included memory-like populations.
- Recurrence cases highlighted immune escape and clinical complexity, including weak vaccine-induced response, low MHC class I expression, and a genetically distinct recurrence.

## What Is Uncertain

- Whether individualized mRNA vaccination improves recurrence-free survival or overall survival in TNBC in randomized trials.
- Which TNBC subtypes, immune states, or residual-disease settings are most likely to benefit.
- Whether neoantigen quality, MHC expression, checkpoint blockade, or other combinations are required.

## Questions For A Clinician Or Trial Team

- Is there a trial for this TNBC stage and treatment history?
- Does the trial require tumor tissue, normal blood, measurable residual disease, or a minimum number of predicted neoantigens?
- Is the trial vaccine-only or combined with checkpoint blockade or other therapy?
- What endpoint is primary: immune response, recurrence-free survival, invasive disease-free survival, safety, or overall survival?
