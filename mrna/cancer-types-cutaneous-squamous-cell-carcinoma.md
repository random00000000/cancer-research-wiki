Cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma is currently a cautionary mRNA tracking page. ## Why This Matters

- Failed, terminated, or non-expanded trials are part of the evidence base.
- A terminated trial can reflect efficacy, safety, feasibility, strategic, recruitment, or design issues; the reason must be investigated before drawing conclusions.
- This is exactly why the wiki must track negative and uncertain approaches, not just promising press releases.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not infer that all V940 programs failed.
- Do not infer the reason for termination without source-backed detail.
- Do not ignore this signal when summarizing the V940 disease landscape.
