# Clinical Endpoints And Evidence Labels For mRNA Cancer Trials

Status: first-pass interpretation page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, patient question preparation, trial landscape review, not for clinical use.

## Summary

mRNA cancer trials can sound impressive while measuring very different things. A T-cell response, tumor shrinkage, delayed recurrence, and longer survival are not interchangeable. Agents should name the endpoint before summarizing the claim.

## Endpoint Translation

| Endpoint | Plain-language meaning | What it can and cannot prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immune response | Blood or tissue tests show T cells recognized vaccine targets. | Shows biological engagement; does not by itself prove patient benefit. |
| Objective response rate | Tumors shrink by defined imaging criteria in a percentage of patients. | Useful in advanced disease; does not always mean durable control or survival. |
| Recurrence-free survival | Time after treatment before cancer returns or death occurs. | Important in adjuvant trials; still needs follow-up and context. |
| Disease-free survival | Time after curative-intent therapy before disease returns, new cancer appears, or death occurs, depending on protocol. | Useful but definitions vary by trial. |
| Progression-free survival | Time before existing cancer grows/spreads or death occurs. | Can support benefit in metastatic disease; not always equal to longer life. |
| Overall survival | Time until death from any cause. | Hardest and often most meaningful, but may take longer and be affected by later therapies. |
| Safety/tolerability | Frequency and severity of adverse events. | Essential for benefit-risk; does not prove anticancer efficacy. |

## Evidence Labels For Agents

- High: replicated phase 3 evidence with clinically meaningful endpoint and regulatory/clinical context.
- Moderate: randomized phase 2 evidence with clinical endpoint and plausible mechanism, awaiting phase 3.
- Low: small uncontrolled human trial, early safety/immune-response study, or incomplete subgroup signal.
- Preliminary: conference abstract, press release, registry result, preprint, or sponsor topline without full peer-reviewed data.
- Preclinical: cell, animal, organoid, or computational evidence only.
- Rejected or unsupported: claim contradicts strong evidence or exceeds available data.

## Current mRNA Examples

- KEYNOTE-942 phase 2b melanoma: moderate for the specific tested setting because it is randomized and uses recurrence-free survival, but still investigational until phase 3/regulatory confirmation.
- TNBC individualized mRNA vaccine report: low-to-preliminary for clinical benefit, but important for immune-durability biology.
- BNT111 topline advanced melanoma: preliminary until full peer-reviewed details are available.
- Active NSCLC, RCC, bladder, and PDAC phase 2/3 trials: trial landscape, not proof of efficacy.

## Safety Boundary

Endpoint literacy helps people ask sharper questions. It does not tell an individual which treatment to choose.
