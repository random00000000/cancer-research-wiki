# mRNA Cancer Vaccines In Melanoma

Status: disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, trial landscape review, patient question preparation, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

Melanoma is the current anchor cancer type for mRNA cancer vaccines. It has the strongest published randomized signal for a personalized neoantigen mRNA therapy, and it also has an off-the-shelf shared-antigen mRNA vaccine program. That does not mean mRNA cures melanoma; it means melanoma is where the clinical evidence is most mature so far.

## Current Evidence

| Program | Setting | What it tests | Current reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KEYNOTE-942 / mRNA-4157/V940 + pembrolizumab | Resected high-risk melanoma | Personalized neoantigen mRNA therapy added to PD-1 blockade | Strongest published randomized mRNA cancer-vaccine signal; still investigational |
| INTerpath-001 / NCT05933577 | Resected high-risk melanoma | Phase 3 confirmation of V940 + pembrolizumab | Active, not recruiting; primary endpoint recurrence-free survival |
| INTerpath-012 / NCT06961006 | Advanced melanoma | V940 + pembrolizumab in unresectable or metastatic disease | Recruiting phase 2; primary endpoint progression-free survival |
| BNT111 / NCT04526899 | Advanced anti-PD-(L)1 relapsed/refractory melanoma | Shared melanoma-antigen mRNA-lipoplex with cemiplimab arms | Completed phase 2; company topline positive, full peer-reviewed detail needed |

## Why Melanoma Matters

- Melanoma often has a high mutation burden, which can create more possible neoantigens.
- Checkpoint inhibitors already matter in melanoma, so adding a vaccine has a plausible combination logic.
- The adjuvant setting after surgery may be more favorable than bulky metastatic disease because there may be fewer cancer cells left to control.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not assume a melanoma result applies to pancreatic, lung, kidney, bladder, or colorectal cancer.
- Do not assume an immune response equals survival benefit.
- Do not assume a phase 2 signal is standard-of-care proof.
- Do not treat a company-reported update as a peer-reviewed final answer.
