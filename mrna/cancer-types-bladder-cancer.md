# mRNA Cancer Vaccines In Bladder Cancer

Status: disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, trial landscape review, patient question preparation, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

Bladder cancer now has multiple V940/intismeran autogene trial questions: muscle-invasive or resected settings with pembrolizumab, and high-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer with BCG. These are important because bladder cancer has immune-sensitive treatment contexts, but the mRNA evidence is still trial-in-progress.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| NCT06833073 / V940-011 | High-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer | V940 + BCG versus BCG alone and related cohorts | Recruiting phase 2; primary endpoint event-free survival |

## Why Bladder Cancer Needs Special Care

- Non-muscle-invasive and muscle-invasive bladder cancer are different clinical situations.
- BCG is a local immunotherapy with its own history, safety profile, and recurrence-prevention logic.
- Combining personalized neoantigen therapy with BCG or pembrolizumab creates different immune and safety questions.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not treat V940 as a replacement for BCG, surgery, cystoscopy, pathology, or clinician-led surveillance.
- Do not merge NMIBC and muscle-invasive settings into one claim.
- Do not infer benefit before disease-specific results are published.
