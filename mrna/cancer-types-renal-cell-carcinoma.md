# mRNA Cancer Vaccines In Renal Cell Carcinoma

Status: disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, trial landscape review, patient question preparation, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

Renal cell carcinoma is a trial-in-progress V940 setting. It is worth tracking because RCC is immunotherapy-relevant, but the mRNA vaccine evidence is not yet a proven RCC treatment.

## Current Evidence

| Program | Setting | What it tests | Current reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NCT06307431 / V940-004 | Renal cell carcinoma after definitive therapy | Adjuvant V940 + pembrolizumab versus placebo + pembrolizumab | Active, not recruiting phase 2; primary endpoint disease-free survival |

## Why RCC Needs Its Own Page

- RCC has a long immunotherapy history, but its mutation and antigen landscape differs from melanoma.
- Disease-free survival after surgery is a different clinical question than shrinking metastatic disease.
- Combination with pembrolizumab complicates attribution of benefit and toxicity.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not assume RCC will follow melanoma results.
- Do not infer benefit before randomized phase 2 data are available.
- Do not treat an adjuvant trial as guidance for metastatic RCC decisions.

## Next Sources To Inspect
