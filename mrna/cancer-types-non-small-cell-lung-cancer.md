# mRNA Cancer Vaccines In Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Status: disease-specific mRNA page
Last updated: 2026-06-14

Audience label: public education, trial landscape review, patient question preparation, not for clinical use.

## Plain Summary

Non-small cell lung cancer is now a major V940/intismeran autogene expansion area, with phase 3 adjuvant and perioperative-style questions plus a newer metastatic squamous NSCLC phase 2 question. These trials are important, but they are mostly registry-stage or trial-in-progress evidence for the mRNA question.

## Why NSCLC Is Different From Melanoma

- Smoking-associated NSCLC can have many mutations, but lung cancer biology, treatment context, and immune escape differ from melanoma.
- NSCLC contains multiple subtypes; squamous and non-squamous disease may not behave the same.
- Standard care can include surgery, radiation, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, and targeted therapy depending on stage and biomarkers.

## What Not To Infer

- Do not automatically transfer melanoma V940 results to NSCLC.
- Do not ignore histology, stage, prior therapy, PD-L1, driver mutations, or surgical context.
- Do not treat trial enrollment as evidence of clinical benefit.

## Next Sources To Inspect

- NCT06077760, NCT06623422, and NCT07221474 updates.
- Subgroup and biomarker plans for squamous versus non-squamous NSCLC.
- Toxicity and feasibility when V940 is combined with pembrolizumab and chemotherapy.
